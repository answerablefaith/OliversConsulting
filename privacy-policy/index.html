Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains how Henry Oliver trading as OliversConsulting.co.uk (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects and uses personal data. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Who we are

Henry Oliver trading as OliversConsulting.co.uk, a sole trader based in London.

  • Address: 3 Yeoman Street, London, SE8 5DF
  • Email: henry@oliversconsulting.co.uk
  • ICO registration reference: ZC195664

What this website does

Oliversconsulting.co.uk is a static information website. It does not collect personal data through forms and does not run analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking. The site’s fonts are stored and served from our own domain.

The site itself does not send us personal information unless you choose to email us. If you follow the booking link, you leave this website and use the separate Cal.eu scheduling service.

The personal data we collect

We hold personal data that you actively provide or that is needed to carry out agreed work:

  • If you email us: your name, email address and anything you choose to include in your message, such as details about your business.
  • If you book a call: the name, email address, details you enter into the scheduling service and the meeting time that is shared with us for the booking.
  • If you become a client: the contact, project and business information needed to scope, deliver, support and invoice the work.

Please do not send special-category personal data or information about children unless we have expressly agreed that it is necessary for a specific project. If a project genuinely requires more sensitive information, the handling requirements should be agreed before it is collected.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry and take steps you request before a contract. Lawful basis: steps at your request before entering a contract, or legitimate interests where appropriate.
  • To deliver agreed services, support the work and communicate about the project. Lawful basis: performance of a contract.
  • To send proposals, invoices and reasonable follow-up connected with our business relationship. Lawful basis: legitimate interests in running the business, or performance of a contract where applicable.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Lawful basis: legal obligation.

Booking a call

Cal.eu is a separate scheduling service reached through an outbound link. If you choose to use it, information you enter is handled on that service under its own privacy terms. We receive the booking details needed to arrange and hold the call. You can instead contact us directly by email.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers where needed to run the business:

  • Cal.eu - scheduling when you choose the booking link.
  • Google Workspace - business email and document storage.

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where our use of a provider involves a restricted international transfer, we are responsible for ensuring that an appropriate UK transfer mechanism, adequacy arrangement or other lawful exception applies. Contact us if you want more detail about the providers used for your information.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become work: normally up to 12 months, unless there is a specific legal or dispute-related reason to keep them longer.
  • Project correspondence and delivery records: for as long as reasonably needed to perform the contract, provide agreed support, handle disputes and meet legal obligations.
  • Accounting and tax records: for the applicable statutory period. HMRC currently requires self-employed business records to be kept for at least five years after the 31 January Self Assessment submission deadline for the relevant tax year. See GOV.UK’s current record-keeping guidance.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis used, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct or erase your personal data, restrict processing, receive certain data in a portable format, or object to processing. The right to object is particularly relevant where processing relies on legitimate interests.

To exercise a right or ask how your data is being used, email henry@oliversconsulting.co.uk. We will respond within the applicable legal timeframe. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Automated decisions and profiling

This website does not make decisions about people using automated profiling, and it does not use personal data for behavioural advertising.

Changes

We review this policy when the website, service providers or data-handling practices change. The current version is the one published here with the date shown above.
