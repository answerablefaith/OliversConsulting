Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar browser storage or access technologies are used in connection with oliversconsulting.co.uk.

Cookies and browser storage on this site

Oliversconsulting.co.uk is a static information website. The current site does not set first-party cookies, does not write browser storage for analytics or profiling, and does not use advertising, behavioural tracking, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.

The website’s fonts are stored and served directly from oliversconsulting.co.uk rather than being requested from an external font provider.

If the site’s use of storage or access technologies changes, this policy will be reviewed and any consent required for non-essential technology will be obtained before that technology is used.

When you book a call

If you follow the booking link, you leave oliversconsulting.co.uk and open a scheduling page provided by Cal.eu. That separate service may use cookies or other storage and access technologies under its own terms. Those technologies are not loaded merely by visiting this website.

Your browser controls

You can block or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. The Information Commissioner’s Office also publishes current guidance on cookies and storage and access technologies.
